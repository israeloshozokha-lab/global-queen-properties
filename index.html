THE RISK IS REAL

LAND PURCHASES REQUIRE MORE THAN JUST MONEY. THEY REQUIRE PROPER VERIFICATION.

Buying land is a major financial decision. When buyers skip important checks, they can run into avoidable problems. Here are common areas that deserve careful attention.

Unclear Seller Ownership

Failing to establish who truly owns the land or has the legal authority to sell it.

Title Not Investigated

Not investigating the property's title or the documents connected to it.

Government Restrictions Ignored

Overlooking government acquisition, planning restrictions, or land-use issues.

Survey and Boundaries Unverified

Buying without verifying the survey plan, boundaries, or the physical location.

Unresolved Disputes

Purchasing land that may have unresolved family, community, or legal disputes.

No Clear Agreement or Records

Paying without a clear written agreement, proper receipts, and good record-keeping.

This is general education, not legal advice. Every land transaction should be independently verified.
